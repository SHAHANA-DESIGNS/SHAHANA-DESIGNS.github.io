About the studio

A practice of quiet ink, careful hands and patient letters.

This portfolio brings together Shahana's original illustrations, handwritten language worksheets, accredited credentials and creative coding experiments.

The work moves between physical and digital worlds: a pencil line can become a learning aid; a worksheet can become a teaching system; a simple web experiment can become a useful little product.
